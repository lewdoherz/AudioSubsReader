Bundled reader fonts (unmodified TrueType files)

Official Google Fonts distribution: https://github.com/google/fonts
Downloaded 2026-10-28. No fonts are fetched at application runtime.
Each family includes regular, italic, bold, and bold italic faces.
Flutter uses local family aliases ReaderSerif, ReaderSans, ReaderMono; the
font files themselves and their internal family names are unmodified.
